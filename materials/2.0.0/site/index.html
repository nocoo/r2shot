What do I need to get started?

Your own Cloudflare R2 bucket, scoped access credentials, and a public domain configured to serve its files. Cloudflare usage charges may apply. R2Shot is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cloudflare.

Where are screenshots and credentials stored?

Screenshot bytes are processed in browser memory and sent directly to your R2 endpoint. The extension keeps no screenshot history. Credentials are stored in plain text in extension-local storage; use keys scoped to the intended bucket. Uploaded files stay in your bucket until you delete them there.

How much of a full page can I capture?

Choose 1–100 viewport heights, with 5 as the default. Images are also limited to 32 million pixels and 32,767 pixels per side. Full-page capture uses loaded content at the current viewport width; sticky elements and dynamic or infinite-scroll pages can affect the result.

What does Test Connection check?

It sends a signed HEAD request to the bucket using the values currently in the form. It does not upload an image, configure public access, or prove the public domain works. Open a link after your first successful upload to verify the complete setup.

Which languages and themes are available?

Light, dark, and system themes. English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, and Russian. The interface follows Chrome's language.
